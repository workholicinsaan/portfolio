HookahPartner · 2026

Mobile rendering rebuilt on an overflowing store

RS Furniture Hub · 2026

The document rendered wider than the viewport, squeezing the site into about two thirds of the phone screen with a horizontal scrollbar and unreadable type. Corrected the viewport meta, swapped fixed pixel container widths for fluid units and max-width, capped media at 100%, and ran an overflow audit to find the element pushing the document past 100vw. Under mobile-first indexing that broken render was the version Google was scoring.

Full device width, no horizontal scroll

Collapsing Shopify's collection-scoped product URLs

Shopify store · 2025

The theme linked products through product.url | within: collection, generating a distinct URL per collection while the canonical pointed elsewhere. Crawl budget went on duplicates and internal link equity split across variants. Removing the filter across the grid, collection, search and related-product templates returned the store to one canonical path per product, with collection context moved into BreadcrumbList schema.

One canonical URL per product
